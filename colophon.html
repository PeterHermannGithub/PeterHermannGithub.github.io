§ 02

The parts

Markup
Hand-written HTML. The header and footer are injected once by a small script, so a new nav tab is one line.
Style
The current design system is one stylesheet on CSS custom properties, layered over an older one that is being retired. Dark by default, light on request, no preprocessor.
Type
Space Grotesk for reading and shouting, Space Mono for everything that is metadata.
Motion
CSS transitions, cross-page view transitions where the browser supports them, and one canvas: the hero. All of it stops under reduced-motion.
Language
English and Hungarian from one dictionary. Nearly every visible string has both and the toggle swaps them without a reload; Anidle and some project-page body copy are still English only.
Search
Client-side. The index is built the first time you focus the box, so an ordinary page load fetches nothing extra. Press / to focus it, or Ctrl K for the menu.
Hosting
GitHub Pages on a custom domain. Deploying is pushing.

§ 03

Decisions, and what they cost

A

No build step

A portfolio that needs an install to change a sentence is a portfolio nobody updates. The cost is real: no minification, no shared components beyond the injected chrome, and a stylesheet that grows by accretion.

B

Two languages from day one

Hungarian is my first language, and the people who most deserve to read this site in it are not the ones who will ask. The cost: nearly every sentence is written twice, and the second one is where the drift hides.

C

The hero does real work

The graph on the home page is a brute-force nearest-neighbour search: distance from your cursor to each word, top five lit. It is the operation the recommender performs, at a size where brute force is the right answer.

D

One accent, used like a highlighter

Acid lime marks what you can act on and what matters most; when it floods a whole band, that band is the point of the page. Everything else is ink on paper. The cost: no colour-coding, so meaning has to come from layout.
