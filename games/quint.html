Quint

Six guesses, five letters, and a vocabulary you can see. You may only guess words from the bank below, so every guess is also a way of crossing words off it. Green is right, amber is in the word, grey is out.

The bank

Every word you may guess, and every word the answer can be. Words with a letter you have ruled out fade.
